## Initial peer policy

This policy was drafted with Codex assistance for Lab 08 before the final candidate admission decisions and calculations. It is not represented as a pre-AI student statement. The September 10 date retains the market date used in the saved Week 3 DCF; a later date would require updating that DCF and all three share prices.

- Seek listed operating companies that sell material data center AI computing accelerators, processors, or networking semiconductors to cloud and enterprise infrastructure buyers. This is the economic exposure to compare, rather than a shared “technology” label.
- Use the same trading-date U.S.-dollar closing share price and the latest annual **reported GAAP total diluted EPS** available by that date. Prices and earnings must refer to compatible post-split common shares. Positive annual EPS is required for a meaningful positive P/E.
- **Use** a candidate if data center AI compute or networking is central to its business and other segments do not dominate the whole-company earnings comparison. **Qualify** a candidate with a material but clearly identified difference in product mix, growth, margin, customer concentration, or software revenue. A qualified peer can provide a sensitivity, but its P/E is not a direct fair-value claim for NVIDIA.
- **Exclude** an enterprise dominated by foundry services, memory, equipment, unrelated software, or another earnings engine, or one lacking verifiable compatible price or annual EPS. Do not change inclusion to obtain a preferred implied price.

Evidence that could reject a candidate: a filing showing the data center business is immaterial, most earnings arise from another activity, nonpositive annual reported earnings, a share-split mismatch, or an unavailable same-date close.

**Independent arithmetic check:** Broadcom's $360.83 / $4.77 = 75.645702...×. Multiplying by NVIDIA's $4.90 gives $370.663941..., displayed as **$370.66**. This is an equity share price directly; the cash and debt bridge belongs to the DCF, not to P/E. Calculations use unrounded multiples and round only displayed results.

**Changed-peer prediction from the model, stated before the check:** AMD has the higher multiple, so removing AMD should lower the two-peer median estimate and leave only Broadcom's single-peer reference, without a range. The executed calculator reports **$370.66**, a **−$280.26/share** change from the unrounded two-peer midpoint. Removing Broadcom instead gives **$931.18**, a **+$280.26/share** change. The saved DCF's reverse calculation used **$218.49**, a Sept. 10 intraday/after-close price observation, while the uniform close in this comparison is **$218.36**. The $0.13 difference does not explain the large disagreement, but the price definitions must remain visible. The DCF and P/E both express common-equity value per diluted share; their assumptions and information windows differ. The peer P/Es are high because September 2026 prices divide by older annual GAAP earnings, and the peers' growth, product mix, and accounting charges differ from NVIDIA's. The DCF is low relative to the Sept. 10 close and uses a high 16% discount rate; its FY2026 starting point omits information published by September 10. The methods should **not** be averaged.

**AI-assisted provisional call: watch-defer, with no defended intrinsic-value range.** At $218.36, the saved DCF sensitivity is far below the observed price, while the peer span is too dispersed and economically mixed to justify initiation by itself. This is a decision to investigate the conflicting assumptions, not proof that NVIDIA is overvalued. The most decision-relevant next evidence is a DCF refreshed with the [Aug. 26, 2026 NVIDIA Q2 FY2027 release](https://nvidianews.nvidia.com/news/nvidia-announces-financial-results-for-second-quarter-fiscal-2027), including cash generation, reinvestment, dilution, and a defensible forward discount rate. That release reports $96.2 billion quarterly revenue and $89.0 billion Data Center revenue; **one quarter is not substituted for annual EPS in this lab**. A refreshed downside-to-upside DCF near or above the price, with credible cash-flow and discount-rate support, could change the call toward initiate. Evidence of slowing profitable Data Center demand or lower sustainable margins could strengthen a do-not-initiate call.

## Skeptical AI review and source-checked judgment

**Codex criticism:** The weakest supported assumption is that a DCF frozen on FY2026 information still represents a September 2026 decision. The peer P/E comparison also treats AMD's roughly 48% Data Center business and Broadcom's 42% infrastructure software revenue as if their consolidated earnings capitalized like NVIDIA's 89.7% Data Center business. The comparison correctly identifies the same company and equity-per-share valuation object, but the DCF uses a $218.49 intraday price rather than the $218.36 close, and the three annual EPS periods end on different dates. **Question:** Would a DCF refreshed with the already-public FY2027 results and a supported discount rate bring NVIDIA's downside case close enough to the market price to justify initiating?

**Judgment: accept the core criticism.** The [NVIDIA FY2027 Q2 release dated Aug. 26](https://nvidianews.nvidia.com/news/nvidia-announces-financial-results-for-second-quarter-fiscal-2027) was public before Sept. 10, while the [saved DCF](lab06_nvda.md) expressly omits later quarters. The [NVIDIA](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1045810/000104581026000021/nvda-20260125.htm), [AMD](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/2488/000000248826000018/amd-20251227.htm), and [Broadcom](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1730168/000173016825000121/avgo-20251102.htm) segment tables support the mix criticism. The same-date [price table](https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/nvda/history/) and the saved DCF support the $0.13 price-definition mismatch; it is minor in size, though still a real definition difference. The criticism does not establish a replacement DCF value. **Answer:** a refreshed, sourced downside case near the observed price could justify reconsidering initiation, but no such refreshed model has been run here. Until then, the conditional call stays watch-defer.
